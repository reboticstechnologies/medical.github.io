Who we are

Creating healthcare and education opportunities that last.

Rebotics Medical & Education Trust is a charitable trust set up to strengthen healthcare delivery, medical education and community health programs. We work with doctors, healthcare workers, educators, partner organisations and volunteers to bring impactful, practical solutions to the communities that need them most.

Our focus is on underprivileged and rural populations, where gaps in access to quality healthcare and training are often the widest.

Our focus areas

Clinical services & camps

Organising periodic health camps, specialty clinics and outreach drives to make primary and preventive care available closer to people.

Education & training

Building the skills of medical students, paramedics and frontline health workers through workshops, fellowships and supported training.

Student support

Providing scholarships, mentorship and academic support to deserving students pursuing medicine and allied health sciences.

Research & innovation

Encouraging outcome‑oriented research and pilots in public health, tele‑medicine and technology‑enabled care.

Governance & compliance

The Trust is committed to following all applicable laws, regulations and best practices for charitable organisations. Governance structures, financial records and statutory filings are maintained carefully and can be shared with relevant authorities and supporters.
