About the Trust

Founded in 2020, Rebotics Medical & Education Trust was born out of a desire to ensure that the "Robotics Revolution" doesn't leave anyone behind.

Our Mission

To provide robotic-assisted medical care to those who cannot afford it and to implement AI-driven educational programs in rural schools.
