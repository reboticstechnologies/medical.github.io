About the Trust

Rebotics Medical & Education Trust is focused on social impact through medical, educational and community-oriented initiatives.

Our Vision

To contribute to a healthier, better educated and more empowered society by making useful services, knowledge and opportunities more accessible.

Our Mission

  • Promote health awareness and preventive wellness.
  • Support education and lifelong learning.
  • Encourage technology and digital literacy.
  • Build partnerships with volunteers and institutions.
  • Design practical programmes for community needs.
