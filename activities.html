Key activities

Programs we run or plan to run as we grow.

Community health camps

Monthly general health camps with basic diagnostics, counselling and follow‑up, focused on underserved neighbourhoods.

Specialised medical clinics

Periodic speciality clinics in collaboration with hospitals and consultants — for example eye, dental, paediatrics or women’s health.

Training workshops

Continuous professional development for nurses, paramedics and other healthcare staff through structured workshops.

Scholarship & mentorship

Financial support and mentoring for students from disadvantaged backgrounds pursuing medical and allied courses.

Public health awareness

Campaigns on hygiene, nutrition, chronic disease prevention, maternal and child health, and mental wellbeing.

Research collaborations

Partnering with institutions and experts to study community health needs and pilot innovative models of care.
