What we do — Activities & Programs

We run a variety of programs across four pillars: Education, Healthcare Delivery, Research & Innovation, and Community Outreach. Below are sample activities you can include on the site.

Education

  • Establish and run medical, nursing and allied health training institutes.
  • Scholarships and tuition support for students from disadvantaged backgrounds.
  • Continuing medical education (CME) workshops for rural healthcare workers.
  • Simulation labs, skill workshops and tele-mentoring programs.

Healthcare Delivery

  • Free and subsidized outpatient clinics and inpatient care.
  • Mobile medical units and telemedicine camps for remote villages.
  • Special medical camps: eye, dental, maternal & child health screenings.
  • Blood donation drives and small-scale community pharmacies.

Research & Innovation

  • Support for clinical & public-health research projects.
  • Medical device prototyping and collaboration with engineering groups.
  • Data-driven community health assessments and publications.

Community Outreach & Preventive Care

  • Vaccination campaigns and health education sessions.
  • Nutrition and maternal health programs.
  • Mental health awareness, counselling and rehabilitation referrals.
  • Sanitation, clean-water initiatives and waste management awareness.

Fundraising & Capacity Building

  • Corporate partnerships and CSR projects.
  • Volunteer programs and internships for students.
  • Fundraising events, donor stewardship and transparency reports.
