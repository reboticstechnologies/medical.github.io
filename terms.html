Terms & Conditions

Key points for use of this website and donations to the Trust.

Purpose of the Trust

Rebotics Medical & Education Trust is a charitable trust established to support healthcare, medical education, research, and related community programs.

Use of Donations

Unless otherwise specified, donations are utilised towards the general objects of the Trust, including ongoing programs, administrative expenses, and future initiatives approved by the trustees.

Receipts & Documentation

Donors are requested to share transaction details so that acknowledgements / receipts can be issued where applicable.

Refund Policy

As a general principle, donations once made are not refundable. However, genuine errors may be reviewed by the trustees on a case‑by‑case basis.

Third‑party Links

Online payment links and integrations may be provided by third‑party service providers. Their terms and privacy policies will apply in addition to ours.

Content Accuracy

While we endeavour to keep information accurate and updated, program details, schedules, and initiatives are subject to change.

Contact Us

For any clarification on these terms or any aspect of the Trust’s work, please email medicaltrust2025@gmail.com.
